How do I arrange home care in Peterborough?

Call our Peterborough office on 01733 808531 or request a callback. We'll discuss the support you need, arrange a home visit to carry out a free care assessment, and then create a care plan built around you.

How quickly can home care start?

Very quickly. We can often start care the next day, and in urgent situations, such as a hospital discharge, sometimes on the same day. If you're planning ahead, we'll work to a timescale that suits you.

How much does home care cost?

It depends on how much support is needed and how often, from a 30-minute visit to full-time live-in care. After your free assessment we'll give you a clear quote, so you know exactly what to expect before anything starts.

Optional: add a guide price, e.g. "Visiting care from £xx an hour". A starting price usually increases enquiries.

Can I get help paying for care?

Many families pay privately, but you may be eligible for support from Peterborough City Council, NHS Continuing Healthcare or Attendance Allowance. We can explain the options when you call. How to pay for care.

Confirm which funding routes Greenwood accepts (self-funded, council-funded, CHC, direct payments).

What happens during a home care assessment?

It's a relaxed conversation in your own home. We'll ask about your routines, your health, the support you already get from family or friends, and what would make the biggest difference day to day. The assessment is free and doesn't commit you to anything. Many families use it simply to understand their options.

What home care services do you provide in Peterborough?

Everything from short visits to help with medication or getting up in the morning, to longer and more frequent visits, night care and live-in care. Support can include personal care, medication, meal preparation, companionship and help around the home. See the types of care.

How flexible is home care once it's set up?

Very flexible. Someone may come home from hospital needing extra visits, or a family member who was helping may no longer be able to. We review and adjust your care plan as needs change, so your support always fits your situation.

Do you only provide elderly care?

No. Elderly care is a large part of what we do, but we also support younger adults and children living with disabilities or long-term conditions, and people recovering after surgery or a hospital stay.

I'm not sure what kind of care we need. Is that a problem?

Not at all, and it's very common. Working that out is a key part of the assessment. We'll explain the options and recommend what would genuinely help, so you don't need to have all the answers before you get in touch.

Do you only cover Peterborough city?

No. We visit within 15 miles of Peterborough, including rural villages, and provide live-in care within 30 miles. Check your town or give us a call.
